## Purpose of This Notice

This Privacy Notice explains how Klassic Inc. ("we," "us," "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information, including **sensitive biometric data**, through our Field Attendance System.

Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to know how your personal data is being processed.

---

## What Personal Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information:

### 1. **Basic Employee Information**
- Full name
- Employee ID/Code
- Email address
- Phone number
- Employment status
- Assigned work site

### 2. **Biometric Information** (Sensitive Personal Information)
- **Facial recognition template**: A mathematical representation of your facial features (NOT a photograph)
- **Liveness detection data**: Signals that confirm you are physically present (e.g., blink detection)

**Important**: We do NOT store your actual facial photographs. We only store a unique numeric "fingerprint" (called an embedding) derived from your face, which cannot be reverse-engineered back into a photo.

### 3. **Location Data**
- GPS coordinates when you check in or check out
- GPS accuracy measurements
- Site location (which work site you're assigned to)

### 4. **Attendance Records**
- Check-in and check-out timestamps
- Device information (phone model, operating system version)
- Offline sync metadata (if you clocked in/out without internet)

---

## Why We Collect This Information (Legal Basis)

We process your personal data for the following lawful purposes:

### 1. **Consent**
- You have provided explicit, written consent to enroll your biometric data
- Consent is specific to timekeeping and payroll purposes
- You may withdraw consent at any time (see "Your Rights" below)

### 2. **Legitimate Business Interest**
- Accurate timekeeping for payroll calculation
- Compliance with labor laws (maintaining employment records)
- Prevention of time theft and "buddy punching"

### 3. **Legal Obligation**
- Compliance with Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE) record-keeping requirements
- Tax and social security reporting obligations

## How We Use Your Information

| Data Type | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **Biometric Template** | Verify your identity during check-in/check-out |
| **GPS Coordinates** | Confirm you are within the designated work site (geofence validation) |
| **Attendance Records** | Calculate worked hours for payroll processing |
| **Audit Logs** | Security monitoring, fraud prevention, dispute resolution |

**We do NOT use your biometric data for:**
- ❌ Marketing or advertising
- ❌ Surveillance outside of timekeeping
- ❌ Sharing with third parties (except as required by law or for payroll processing)
- ❌ Profiling or automated decision-making beyond attendance verification

## Who Has Access to Your Information

Access to your personal data is strictly limited to:

| Role | Access Level | Purpose |
|------|--------------|---------|
| **You (Employee)** | Your own attendance records | Review your own history |
| **HR Personnel** | Biometric enrollment, attendance records | Employee management, payroll processing |
| **Supervisors** | Attendance records for their team | Oversight, exception handling |
| **Payroll System** | Verified attendance records only (no biometric data) | Salary calculation |
| **IT/Security Staff** | System logs, encrypted database | Technical support, security monitoring |
| **Data Protection Officer** | All data (for compliance review only) | Ensuring legal compliance |

**All access is logged and audited.**

## How We Protect Your Information (Security Measures)

We implement the following safeguards:

### Technical Safeguards
- ✅ **Encryption**: Your facial recognition template is encrypted both in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (AES-256)
- ✅ **Access Control**: Role-based access (only authorized personnel can view data)
- ✅ **Audit Logging**: Every access to your data is logged and monitored
- ✅ **Multi-Factor Authentication**: Required for HR/admin accounts
- ✅ **Secure Servers**: Data stored in ISO 27001 / SOC 2 certified facilities

### Organizational Safeguards
- ✅ **Privacy Training**: All staff handling personal data are trained on RA 10173
- ✅ **Data Protection Officer**: Independent oversight of compliance
- ✅ **Incident Response Plan**: Procedures for handling data breaches
- ✅ **Regular Security Audits**: Annual penetration testing and compliance reviews

### Physical Safeguards
- ✅ **On-Device Processing**: Liveness detection happens on your phone (not sent over the network)
- ✅ **No Photo Storage**: We never store your enrollment photo; only the mathematical template

## Data Retention (How Long We Keep Your Information)

| Data Type | Retention Period | Why |
|-----------|------------------|-----|
| **Facial Recognition Template** | **30 days after employment ends** OR immediately upon consent withdrawal | DPA requirement to minimize sensitive data retention |

| **Audit Logs** | **1 year** | Security monitoring, compliance review |

**After the retention period ends, your data is permanently deleted from our systems.**

## Your Rights Under the Data Privacy Act

You have the following rights:

### 1. **Right to Access**
- Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
- Response time: Within 30 days

### 2. **Right to Correction**
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information
- Example: Updating your phone number or email address

### 3. **Right to Erasure**
- Request deletion of your biometric template
- **Note**: Attendance records may still be retained per labor law requirements, but your facial recognition template will be deleted immediately

### 4. **Right to Object**
- Object to the processing of your biometric data
- **Alternative available**: Supervisor-verified manual check-in (see below)

### 5. **Right to Data Portability**
- Request your attendance records in a machine-readable format (CSV/JSON)

### 6. **Right to Withdraw Consent**
- You may withdraw your consent to biometric processing at any time
- **Effect**: Your facial recognition template will be deleted within 7 days
- **Alternative**: You will be switched to the manual check-in process

## Non-Biometric Alternative

**You are NOT required to use biometric timekeeping.** If you object to facial recognition, you may opt for:

**Alternative Method**: Supervisor-Verified Manual Check-In
- You report your check-in/check-out to your immediate supervisor
- Supervisor logs your attendance in the system on your behalf
- Same payroll processing, no biometric data collection

**To request the alternative method**, contact HR or the DPO.

## Data Breach Notification

In the unlikely event of a data breach affecting your personal information:

1. **We will notify you** within **72 hours** of discovering the breach (if the breach poses a risk to your rights)
2. **We will notify the National Privacy Commission (NPC)** within **72 hours**
3. **We will take immediate action** to contain the breach and prevent further unauthorized access

**What you should do if notified:**
- Follow instructions provided in the breach notification
- Monitor for unusual activity (identity theft, unauthorized payroll changes)
- Contact the DPO with any concerns
